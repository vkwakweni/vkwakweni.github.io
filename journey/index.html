Platform1

Rolling

BSc Computer Science thesis, University of Pécs (Hungary), 2026

Delving into sports science using Ethics by design

With small data, descriptive over prescriptive.

  1. The problem: small, domain-specific datasets, where big methods overreach.
  2. The first case: menstrual pain and hormone levels in elite athletes, and whether they relate to performance.
  3. The design: an analysis engine with no web or database code, built for this study, with room to grow.
  4. The rule: the optional AI step only ever sees the aggregated results.

LessonAlways sanity check your logic.

OpenAIOllama

In the product: an optional step that sends only aggregated results to OpenAI or a local Ollama model, to rewrite the report.

Building it: I built the analysis to be mathematically sound, and AI makes the results easy to read.

Municipal expenditure explained to residents.

177 official documents · 30,725 pagesfrom 9 municipalities, 2021 to 2024; 6 ingested so far

  1. The idea: answers about municipal expenditure that you can trace and trust.
  2. The catch: 23 empty tables turned out to be the main financial statements, pasted in as pictures.
